# The Program

Three tiers, matched to training age. Start at the tier your Pass 2 classification puts you in. Do not skip ahead — the tiers aren't difficulty levels, they're matched to how fast you can still recover and progress, and a novice on an advanced program progresses more slowly, not faster.

Every set listed is a **working set**. Warm up before the first exercise of each pattern: two or three progressively heavier sets of 3–5 reps, ending around 80% of your working weight.

Rest **2–3 minutes** between sets of the big compounds, **60–90 seconds** on isolation work. Cutting rest short to save time costs you reps on the next set, which costs you the progression, which costs you the point. If you're short on time, cut exercises, not rest.

---

## Tier 1 — Full body, 3 days a week

For: untrained and novice, states 1–4. Run it for at least 6 months. Days are non-consecutive — Monday / Wednesday / Friday, or any spacing with a rest day between.

Alternate A and B. Week one is A-B-A, week two is B-A-B.

### Workout A

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Back squat | 3 × 5 | The anchor lift. Learn it properly. |
| Bench press | 3 × 5 | |
| Barbell row | 3 × 8 | Chest-supported row is a fine substitute |
| Lat pulldown or chin-up | 3 × 8–12 | Chin-ups if you can do 5+ |
| Romanian deadlift | 2 × 10 | Hamstrings and lower back |
| Ab wheel or hanging leg raise | 3 × 8–15 | |

### Workout B

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Deadlift | 2 × 5 | Fewer sets — it costs the most to recover from |
| Overhead press | 3 × 5 | |
| Front squat or leg press | 3 × 8 | |
| Seated cable row | 3 × 10 | |
| Dumbbell curl | 3 × 10–12 | |
| Triceps pushdown or dip | 3 × 10–12 | |

**Two days a week of conditioning, separate from lifting or after it.** One low-intensity — a brisk 20–30 minute walk, easy cycling. One higher-intensity — 10–15 minutes of intervals, hill sprints, or a circuit. This is for cardiovascular health, not fat loss; fat loss is decided in the kitchen. Do not skip it because it doesn't build muscle.

## Tier 2 — Upper / lower, 4 days a week

For: intermediates, roughly 12–18 months in, when Tier 1 progression has slowed to monthly. Mon/Tue rest Thu/Fri, or any two-on one-off pattern.

### Upper A (strength emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Bench press | 4 × 5 |
| Barbell row | 4 × 6 |
| Overhead press | 3 × 8 |
| Chin-up | 3 × 8 |
| Lateral raise | 3 × 12–15 |
| Triceps extension | 3 × 10–12 |

### Lower A (strength emphasis)

| Exercise | Sets × reps |
|---|---|
| Back squat | 4 × 5 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8 |
| Leg press | 3 × 10 |
| Leg curl | 3 × 12 |
| Standing calf raise | 4 × 12 |
| Ab work | 3 × 12 |

### Upper B (hypertrophy emphasis)

## Tier 3 — Push / pull / legs, 5–6 days a week

For: solid intermediates with the recovery capacity to support it — which is the constraint, not the enthusiasm. Only move here if your Response Profile says fast recovery and high volume tolerance, and if your sleep is genuinely good.

Run push / pull / legs / rest / push / pull / legs, or the six-day version if you can hold it.

**Push:** bench press 4×6, overhead press 3×8, incline dumbbell press 3×10, lateral raise 4×15, overhead triceps extension 3×12, pushdown 3×12.

**Pull:** deadlift 3×5 (first pull day only; second pull day starts with rows), barbell row 4×8, pulldown 4×10, face pull 3×15, barbell curl 3×10, hammer curl 3×12.

**Legs:** squat 4×6, Romanian deadlift 3×8, leg press 3×12, leg curl 3×12, calf raise 4×15, ab work 3×15.

Most people who jump to Tier 3 too early do not need more volume. They need to eat more and sleep more. Six days of unrecovered training produces less than three days of recovered training.

## Substitutions

If your gym lacks equipment, or a lift hurts, swap within the pattern — never drop the pattern.

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Back squat | Front squat, hack squat, leg press, goblet squat, split squat |
| Deadlift | Trap-bar deadlift, Romanian deadlift, back extension, hip thrust |
| Bench press | Dumbbell press, machine chest press, weighted push-up, dip |
| Overhead press | Dumbbell press seated, landmine press, machine shoulder press |
| Barbell row | Dumbbell row, chest-supported row, cable row, inverted row |
| Chin-up | Assisted pull-up, lat pulldown, banded pull-up |

## What to log

Every session, three columns: **exercise, weight, reps completed per set.** Optionally a fourth: RIR on the last set. Nothing else. A log you actually maintain beats an elaborate spreadsheet you abandon in week three.

The log is not paperwork. It is the instrument. Without it you cannot apply the next chapter at all.
