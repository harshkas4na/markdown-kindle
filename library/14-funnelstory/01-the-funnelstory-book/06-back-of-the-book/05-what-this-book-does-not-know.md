# What this book does not know

A good book says where it is thin. Here is where this one is, as of 11 November 2026.

## It was built from the record, not from a briefing

Every fact came from the company folder in work-ops (decisions, customers, glossary, people, course units), from the product and company notes, and from the GreyOrange study notes. Nobody has walked you through an account. So the book inherits the record's gaps.

## The record itself is a bit stale

- The current-state notes were last rewritten on 10 November.
- Slack was last swept 48 days ago (24 September), so channel facts after that come from standups and your own pastes only.
- More than 100 action items from calls are still open in the ledger, so "what is open" in the customer chapters may be out of date.
- The Nested Cognition side is out of scope for this book on purpose.

## Facts that are marked heard or unconfirmed

- Brent's surname and spelling, Shoaib's surname, several GreyOrange spellings (Neelesh or Nilesh).
- Trellix product names HX, DX and ePO.
- Whether Workstream, Docyt and Popmenu are live.
- The Showpad cohort percentages (three inconsistent versions).
- DocuSign's envelope utilization figures.
- The status of the GreyOrange go-live report since September.

## Things the book simplifies

- **Prediction.** The real settings and weights change per workspace. The numbers shown are from one script.
- **The flow function census.** It is from 3 September across four workspaces. New functions have appeared since.
- **The code size numbers.** From the first week. The back-end moves about 100 commits a week.
- **Customer chapters 4.5 to 4.8.** Thinner than GreyOrange's, because that account is yours and has the richest notes.

## What I would check before saying any number to a customer

1. The date on the note it came from.
2. Whether it was verified (you ran or read it) or assumed.
3. Whether it is still true in the live workspace.
4. The denominator, if it is a rate.

## How to improve it

- After every call where a word or a fact was new, add it to the glossary and to the right chapter. The book is only as good as the record it reads.
- When a chapter is wrong, fix the record first, then the chapter.
- When you answer a check question correctly out loud, tick it off in your study log. When you cannot, make it a lab topic.
